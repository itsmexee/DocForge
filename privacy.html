Privacy Policy

1. Zero File Retention (Client-Side Processing)

OmniTool processes all document mergers and image edits directly inside your web browser using client-side JavaScript. Your files are never uploaded, transferred, or stored on external servers or databases.

We store minimal non-identifiable preference data, such as daily usage credits, locally in your device's browser storage. This data remains on your machine and is never sold.
